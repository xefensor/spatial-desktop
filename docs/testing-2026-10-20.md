## Issues found and corrected

1. **Pointer resizing in Edit layout:** widget Edit covers painted above panel borders and intercepted their pointer input. The panel body now establishes a local content stacking context beneath both thickness and length resize handles. Regression coverage checks this relationship on every edge, compact and expanded, including a Spacer.
2. **Briefly disabled controls after Overview:** closing Overview updated the native modal state, but compact launchers, Clock and Overview's expanded state could wait for the next one-second refresh. Overview now refreshes panel projections immediately after its own state is updated. The regression exercises both Work and General with no timer or animation-frame flush.

This is a desktop Chrome smoke test, not cross-browser or mobile certification. It exercises the initial Guide chapters rather than every lesson, and does not verify native OS integration or an external AI service.

Two interaction faults were identified during this walkthrough. Desktop-indicator rails did not route wheel input to desktop navigation; scrolling over a non-scrollable part of a panel provided a working alternative. The Project strip also intercepted the centers of title-bar controls, so the affected parking and maximize steps required clicks on the exposed lower part of those controls. These observations prompted fixes; they are not evidence that the revised implementation has already passed a live retest.

The accompanying Guide pass shortens lesson copy, names visible menu paths, makes detailed help available within active lessons, and preserves open disclosures, focus and scroll during updates. It also updates Show me targets for native panels, recognizes searches that return existing app instances, improves the compact and small-screen layout, and protects introduction feature gates from component-style overrides. Existing lesson identifiers and saved-progress compatibility remain intact. The Project-strip regression covers left, top and hidden panels, window-control placement, both column types and removal of the final Project; rail-wheel checks cover both rails, icon targets, Shift, scroll ownership and navigation guards. Live verification follows publication.
